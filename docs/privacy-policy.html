Privacy Policy

We believe privacy is a right, not a feature. Here's exactly how ClawdKit handles your data.

No data collection · 100% local processing

Last updated: June 2025  ·  Effective immediately

The short version

  • ✓ ClawdKit does  not  collect, store, or transmit any personal data to any external server.
  • ✓ All conversation data stays entirely within your browser — nothing ever leaves your device.
  • ✓ The extension only reads your Claude.ai session to perform actions you explicitly request.
  • ✓ No analytics, no tracking, no ads — ever.

1 Overview

ClawdKit ("the Extension") is a browser extension for Claude.ai that lets you export conversations, count tokens, track usage limits, and browse your conversation history — entirely within your own browser.

This Privacy Policy describes how ClawdKit handles information when you install and use the Extension. By using ClawdKit, you agree to this policy.

2 Data We Do Not Collect

ClawdKit never collects or transmits any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, or any account information
  • Your Claude.ai conversation content or messages
  • Your Claude.ai authentication tokens or session cookies
  • Usage statistics, token counts, or limit data
  • IP address, location, or device identifiers
  • Browsing history or activity on any site
  • Any exported files you create with the Extension

3 Local Data Processing

To provide its features, ClawdKit reads data from the Claude.ai website using your existing authenticated browser session. This is the same data you see when you use Claude.ai normally. All processing happens exclusively within your browser:

  • Conversation export reads your Claude.ai API on your behalf and writes files directly to your device
  • Token counting runs a local tokenizer algorithm in-page — no external API calls are made
  • Usage bar data is read from Claude's native session endpoints, same as the Claude.ai website itself
  • User preferences (theme, export format, etc.) are stored in your browser's local chrome.storage / browser.storage — only accessible by you

4 Browser Permissions Explained

ClawdKit requests the minimum permissions needed to function. Here's exactly what each permission is used for:

Permission Why it's needed
activeTab Detect when you're on a Claude.ai tab so the extension activates correctly.
storage Save your preferences (theme, export settings, org ID) locally in your browser.
scripting Inject the token counter and usage bar UI elements into the Claude.ai page.
tabs Open the conversation browse page in a new tab when you request it.
host: claude.ai Read your Claude.ai conversations and usage data using your existing authenticated session — this never leaves your browser.

5 Third-Party Services

ClawdKit does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services (e.g., Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Sentry).

The Extension uses the following open-source libraries, all of which run entirely client-side within your browser:

  • JSZip — creates ZIP archives of exported conversations locally in your browser
  • tiktoken (o200k_base) — tokenizer used for approximate token counting, runs fully in-page

No data is sent to the authors of these libraries. No network requests are made by these libraries.

6 Children's Privacy

ClawdKit is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you believe a child has used the Extension, please note that since we collect no data at all, there is nothing to delete.

7 Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy changes in any material way, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify users via the Extension's GitHub repository. Continued use of the Extension after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

Because ClawdKit collects no personal data, any future changes are very unlikely to affect your privacy in any meaningful way.
